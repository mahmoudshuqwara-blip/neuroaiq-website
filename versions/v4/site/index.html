UnderstandThe LEAD™ framework

Adoption is not an individual choice. It is a chain of consequences.

Leadership decisions shape organisational conditions. Those conditions influence how people respond and adapt. That response determines adoption, and adoption determines business value. LEAD is how we locate the break in that chain.

  1. L

    Leadership Decisions

    What gets funded, mandated and modelled at the top.

  2. E
