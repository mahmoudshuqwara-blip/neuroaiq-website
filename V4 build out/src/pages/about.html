About NeuroAIQ

We help organisations get greater value from the AI they have already paid for.

NeuroAIQ sits at the intersection of neuroscience, workforce AI adoption and business value. Founded in 2025 in Melbourne, Victoria.

Technology does not create value on its own. The conditions around it decide whether any of the investment is returned.

NeuroAIQ was founded to answer a question most organisations can’t answer with the data they already hold. What’s actually happening between an AI investment and the business value it was expected to produce.

We are not a change management provider, and we do not run the programme. We measure the organisational and workforce conditions that decide whether AI is adopted at all, and we give leaders the evidence to see where value is being created, where it is being lost, and what to prioritise next.
