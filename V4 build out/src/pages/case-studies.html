Case study · DRIVE™ Adaptability Diagnostic

The barriers metrics never showed.

We partnered with a management team struggling with adoption to understand why it kept stalling. Through our diagnostic, we identified 62 operational pain points their metrics never showed, and traced the organisational conditions holding adoption back.

3

Brain-response patterns mapped

  1. Challenge

    Why adoption stalled

    A management team was struggling with adoption. We partnered with them to find the cause.

  2. Key findings

    62 hidden barriers

    We identified 62 operational pain points their metrics never showed, the organisational environment holding adoption back.

  3. What the assessment revealed

    Mapped to the brain

    Using the LEAD™ framework, we mapped these to the workforce’s adaptive brain responses, and identified the three patterns driving low adoption.

  4. Business implications

    Structural, not resistance

    68% of the low adoption came from structural organisational conditions, not individual resistance to change.

  5. Outcome

    Behaviour, not awareness

    By Day 16, 78% of participants were still using our tools, a shift in behaviour, not just awareness, moving toward stronger adoption.
