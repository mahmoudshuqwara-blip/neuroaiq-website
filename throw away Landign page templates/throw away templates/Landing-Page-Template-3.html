01 — The framework

AI programs don't fail on technology. They fail on absorption.

Capability compounds; organisations adapt linearly. The distance between the two is where transformation budgets disappear. Four disciplines, converging — and the name is the method.

  • Neuro The human layer

    Plasticity, threat-response and psychological safety — why teams resist, and what makes new behaviour stick.

  • AI The capability layer

    Use-case prioritisation through to workflow deployment, with guardrails and evidence of value.

  • AQ The velocity layer

    How fast your organisation absorbs change. We baseline it by function, then lift it.

  • IQ The systems layer

    Governance, architecture and business logic, so every AI decision stays defensible.

  • 02

    Pathways strengthen through spaced, contextual practice, not one-off workshops.

04 — Method

Four stages. They overlap, because transformation does.

  • Weeks 1–4

    Assess & decode

    Cognitive and AI readiness diagnostic. Capability, data maturity, resistance patterns, AQ baseline.

  • Weeks 3–8

    Rewire & align

    Leadership alignment and neuroscience-based communication. One narrative, less perceived threat.

  • Weeks 6–16

    Integrate & upskill

    Workflow integration with role-specific training. Capability built inside live work.

  • Week 14 on

    Sustain & evolve

    Reinforcement and tracking. Adoption moves into business reporting, then we hand over.
