Our Approach

The LEAD™ Framework

LEAD™ is NeuroAIQ’s neuroscience-informed framework for identifying and measuring barriers to successful AI adoption.

L

Leadership Decisions

Leadership decisions shape the environment for AI adoption.

Examples

Work design · Governance · Communication · Priorities · Accountability

Outcome

Creates the environment where AI is introduced.

▼
E

Organisational Environment

People respond to the environment, not the technology.

Examples

Clarity · Psychological safety · Workload · Autonomy · Support · Culture

Outcome

Shapes how people experience AI.

▼
A

Adaptive Brain Responses

The brain decides before behaviour changes.

Brain responses

Trust · Uncertainty · Cognitive load · Perceived control · Motivation · Reward

Outcome

Shapes how people adopt AI.

▼
D

Direction of Adoption

Human behaviour determines whether AI delivers value.

AI is adopted when people
  • Use it confidently
  • Integrate it into everyday work
  • Develop new habits
  • Improve performance
AI is resisted when people
  • Avoid using it
  • Use it inconsistently
  • Return to old ways of working
  • Work around the technology
Outcome

AI is either adopted or resisted across the workforce.

Organisations typically engage us before or during significant AI initiatives, when leaders need confidence that AI investments will deliver measurable business value.
