Privacy

We collect only what we need to reply to you, and we don't sell or share it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry or book a conversation: your name, work email, organisation, and optionally your phone number, role, organisation size, AI adoption stage and what you'd like to discuss.

Why

To reply to you and to prepare for a conversation you've asked for. Nothing else.

Where it's kept

Enquiries are delivered to NeuroAIQ's business email and customer records (Microsoft 365 and/or HubSpot). They are not stored on the web server.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are useful, measured in aggregate. Enquiry details are never sent to analytics or advertising platforms.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@neuroaiq.com.au.

If something goes wrong

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and, where the Privacy Act 1988 applies, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
